# Space Werewolf v1.2.0 Update Announcement

## Update Overview

This update brings a brand new **Ruin Cleaning System**, multiple interface optimizations, and game balance adjustments, significantly improving the gaming experience and visual effects.

---

## New Features

### 🏚️ Ruin Cleaning System

**Ruin Generation After Bomb Explosion:**
- When a bomb explodes in any room, that room becomes in "ruin state"
- Ruined rooms cannot execute normal tasks
- 🏚️ marker and gray background displayed on the map

**Cleaning Ruins Task:**
- Crew members need to click the "Clean Ruins" button in ruined rooms
- Drag debris into the trash can to complete cleaning
- Room functions restored after cleaning
- Cannot initiate emergency meeting when the Spaceship Hall is ruined

### 🔧 Sabotage System Cooldown

- Sabotage system now has a **2-round cooldown**
- Cooldown starts after sabotaging oxygen or monitoring systems
- Remaining rounds displayed during cooldown
- Log notification when cooldown ends

### ⚠️ Emergency Meeting Disable Mechanism

- Emergency meeting disabled for **2 rounds** when bomb explodes in Spaceship Hall
- Red warning label and cooldown progress bar displayed during disable
- Automatically restored after cooldown ends
